Privacy Policy – Ashdod Port

Source: ashdodport.co.il – Privacy Policy

Ashdod Port Company Ltd. ("Ashdod Port", "we" or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of users of Ashdod Port's website at https://www.ashdodport.co.il ("the Website") and all services offered on the website for users ("User/s", "you"). This policy also applies where you are directed to it in the context of receiving Services from Ashdod Port.

This Privacy Policy ("the Privacy Policy") has been created to inform you about the way we process, collect, store and use the Personal Information and Non-Personal Information (as defined below) that you provide us through the Website or in the context of receiving Services from Ashdod Port. In addition, the Privacy Policy below describes the measures we take to secure the information collected by us and how you can contact us regarding this policy.

Use of the Website and the services offered therein and/or provision of Personal Information by the User, as applicable, constitutes your consent to the collection of Personal Information and the use of Personal Information by Ashdod Port for the purposes detailed in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with its other terms.

1. Definitions and Interpretation:

1.1. "Personal Information" – data relating to an identified individual or an individual who can be identified with reasonable effort, directly or indirectly. This is information that you knowingly provide, with your consent, for the purpose of receiving information and services presented on the Website.

1.2. "Non-Personal Information" – information that, by itself, does not enable identification of or contact with you. This is technical information or anonymous information or statistical and aggregated information that, as stated, does not enable identification of the User using reasonable efforts. For example, device type, browser type, operating system, language, as well as statistical information related to the scope of use of the Website including date and time of use, duration of use, usage history, pages viewed, including pages that linked Users to the Website. Subject to the provisions of law, Ashdod Port may use this information without limitation, and it is primarily used for the purpose of operating the Website, analyzing the use of the Website, improving the Website, its content and user experience, for statistics and internal analysis purposes, and for improving Ashdod Port's marketing efforts.

1.3. The division in this Privacy Policy into sections and section headings is for convenience only and shall not be used for interpretation purposes.

1.4 Everything stated in this Privacy Policy in the singular also means the plural.

2. General:

2.1. The scope of this Privacy Policy is limited only to information collected through your use of the Website's services (whether such use is through any computer used to access the Website's services, including without limitation, a desktop computer, laptop, mobile phone or any other electronic device) or in the context of services you receive from Ashdod Port where you have been directly referred to the Privacy Policy.

2.2. Collection of Personal Information and its use are carried out in accordance with applicable law, including the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 ("the Privacy Protection Law"). Ashdod Port is the "Database Controller" or “Controller" (in accordance with the definition of "controller" in the Privacy Protection regulations) with respect to Personal Information collected in the context of use of the Website and related services. For any question related to the Privacy Policy, collection and use of Personal Information and exercise of your rights regarding Personal Information, you may contact us using the contact methods detailed at the bottom of this policy.

2.3. The User is under no legal obligation to provide Personal Information. Provision of Personal Information as stated depends on the User's will and consent. However, provision of certain Personal Information may be necessary for the purpose of fulfilling the purpose of its collection, as detailed under this Privacy Policy. If you choose not to provide this information, you will not be able to receive the requested response or service, or they may be provided at a reduced quality. For example, if you wish to contact us to receive answers to certain questions, this cannot be done without receiving contact details.

2.4. By choosing to provide Personal Information to Ashdod Port, you represent and undertake that the Personal Information you have provided or will provide to Ashdod Port is complete and accurate. In the event of any change or update to the information, you undertake to update Ashdod Port. It is clarified to you, and you agree that the Personal Information will be stored in Ashdod Port's databases and that it may process it and transfer it to third parties as detailed in the Privacy Policy.

2.5. This Privacy Policy shall be governed by the laws of the State of Israel and any dispute shall be adjudicated in the competent courts in Tel Aviv-Jaffa.

3. How Does Ashdod Port Collect Personal Information?

3.1. Customer Login to the Website: If you are a registered customer, you will need to enter an ID number and password to gain access to the personal area. Login to the personal area is performed through registration via the Israel Ports Company website; login credentials for entering the personal area are not stored by Ashdod Port.

3.2. Invoice Payment: Through the Website, invoices can be paid. When making payment, you will be asked to provide Personal Information to the company providing us with credit clearing services: full name of the credit card holder used to make the payment, credit card number, expiration date, bank name, payment terms. Ashdod Port does not have access to payment details and they are not stored by Ashdod Port.

3.3. Completing Safety Tests for Visitors to Ashdod Port: The Website has a safety training system intended for drivers, contractors, suppliers and customers entering the gates of Ashdod Port, at the end of which is a questionnaire. For this purpose, you will be asked to fill in Personal Information including full name, ID number, telephone, mobile phone, type of occupation, passport, email, company name and address.

3.4. Receiving Various Services from Ashdod Port: As part of the port's operations, information about users is collected in various ways, including through online forms, booking systems, cameras, direct communication with Ashdod Port representatives, and more.

3.5. Contact Us: If you wish to contact us, Personal Information relating to you and your inquiry will be collected in Ashdod Port's databases. You can contact us in the following ways:

3.5.1. Contact through the Website. As part of contacting through the Website, you will be asked to specify the reason for the inquiry, first name, email and telephone for contact. In addition, you may specify additional details to improve handling of your inquiry.

3.5.2. Telephone call to *8020 or to one of the telephone numbers appearing in the telephone list on the Website.

3.5.3. Contact through customer service on the "WhatsApp" platform.

3.5.4. Sending a complaint form to the address of Ashdod Port Company Ltd., P.O. Box 9001 Ashdod, postal code 77191. As part of filling out the complaint form, you will be asked to fill in the following details: title, full name, ID number, full address, telephone number, email address and the nature of the complaint.

4. Purposes of Collection and Use of Information:

4.1. We may use your Personal and Non-Personal Information for the purpose of providing the Website's services, managing it, maintaining it, solving technical problems, improving user experience, ensuring proper operation of the service, and contacting you, as necessary.

4.2. We use Personal Information for the purpose of accessing the personal area and providing services on the Website as detailed above. Among other things, you will be able to view reports and details relating to containers, complete safety test questionnaires and pay invoices.

4.3. Subject to the provisions of law, Ashdod Port Company (directly and/or through others) will use this information to send the User mailings, information and marketing materials to which they have subscribed, including in the context of direct mail.

4.4. We use the Personal Information provided by you for the purpose of handling and examining your requests, inquiries or for the various services that Ashdod Port provides to you.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information to Third Parties:

5.1. We may share Personal Information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating the Website, managing our business or providing our services, such as clearing services used to transfer payments for our customers. These third parties may use your Personal Information only to the extent necessary to perform the services they provide for us, and they are obligated to take security measures consistent with the law to maintain confidentiality and protect your Personal Information.

5.2. We may also share your information with analytics service providers for analytics services. Such analytics service providers use cookies or similar tools on your computer, through which they can collect information about your use of our Website. This helps us compile aggregated statistical data regarding the effectiveness of our Website and services.

5.3. Sharing Information with Public Authorities and Security Bodies – Ashdod Port may be required by law to share Personal Information with public authorities for various purposes (for example, transferring information to the Shipping and Ports Authority, border control and customs authorities, entry requirements, prevention of terrorism or any other crime, or protection of public health). In addition, Ashdod Port may transfer information at the request of public bodies through the information transfer mechanism between public bodies, as regulated in the Privacy Protection Law.

5.4. You understand and agree that we may be required to disclose Personal Information about you if we believe that such disclosure is necessary to avoid legal liability, is required in the context of compliance with legal proceedings (including, but not limited to, a subpoena, search warrant or court order), to protect our rights or property or the rights and property of a third party, to maintain public safety or that of a particular individual, or to prevent or stop illegal, unethical activity, or activity that could lead to legal action. In addition, we may sell, transfer or share some or all our assets, including your Personal Information, in the context of processes related to organizational change, including merger, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets or in the event of bankruptcy.

5.5. Except as detailed in this Privacy Policy, the Personal Information you provide will not be transferred to third parties.

5.6. The Company may share information with third parties when "cookies" and monitoring tools from them are used for online advertising purposes – as detailed in the Privacy Policy (see "Use of Cookies and Monitoring Tools").

6. Data Subject Rights:

6.1. Ashdod Port respects your privacy and your control over your Personal Information. In accordance with the Privacy Protection Law, your rights regarding your Personal Information held in Ashdod Port's database are as follows:

6.1.1. You may request to access your Personal Information held by Ashdod Port.

6.1.2. You may request correction and/or deletion of your Personal Information if you find that the Personal Information is incorrect and/or not up to date.

6.1.3. If you do not wish to receive marketing mailings, you may, at any time, request your removal from the mailing list through the "removal" option in the marketing message sent to you and/or through the link on the Company's website and/or in accordance with other instructions that will appear in the mailing received.

6.2. You may request to exercise your rights above by contacting the Data Protection Officer at the email address Dpo@ashdodport.co.il or by contacting us using the contact methods appearing on the Website and detailed in the Privacy Policy. When contacting to correct and/or access and/or delete Personal Information as stated above, and for the purpose of protecting users' privacy, Ashdod Port may request certain details for identification purposes before providing a response and/or fulfilling the request.

6.3. It is clarified that information required by Ashdod Port for the purpose of managing the port - including documentation of actions performed in connection with a service provided - will continue to be stored by the operator in accordance with law, but will no longer be used for contacting you by direct mail and/or for any other purpose except compliance with legal provisions and/or protection of Ashdod Port's rights under law.

7. Duration of Personal Information Retention:

7.1. Ashdod Port will retain your Personal Information only for the period of time required for the purposes for which it is processed and in accordance with applicable law requirements. We will review the Personal Information in our possession from time to time, and if we find that there is no legal, business or customer service-related need to continue storing it – we will delete it securely or, in certain cases, anonymize it.

7.2. However, Ashdod Port may retain your Personal Information for a longer period in the following cases: (a) when the law or instruction of a regulatory authority requires this; (b) when Ashdod Port is contractually obligated to do so; (c) when the information is required for legal defense purposes or for managing disputes.

8.1. Ashdod Port takes standard security measures to maintain the confidentiality of data subjects' information, protect their privacy and reduce the risk of intrusion and unauthorized use of Personal Information; however, absolute protection cannot be guaranteed and there may be security breaches and intrusions into systems that are beyond Ashdod Port's reasonable control.

8.2. Ashdod Port may store Personal Information both in Israel and in other countries in the world and/or allow access to Personal Information from countries outside Israel. The User hereby confirms that when using the platforms, their Personal Information may be transferred, stored and processed outside the State of Israel. Transfer of Personal Information outside the borders of Israel will be done in accordance with applicable law.

8.3. If you have identified security gaps or wish to report a security incident, please contact us at the email address ILANM@ashdodport.co.il or by contacting us using the contact methods appearing on the Website and detailed in the Privacy Policy.

If you are not sure how to do this, you can check the instructions in the help file of the browser you are using.
